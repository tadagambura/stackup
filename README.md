# StackUp

**Savings challenges you actually finish**

> #1 of 33 in the build order · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium–high · Money & Work
> **Build first · growth-engine pilot**

## What it is

Pick a savings challenge (52-week, £5-a-day, £1,000 in 90 days, 100 envelopes, no-spend weekends), log what you put away, and watch the stack grow. No bank connection: you log deposits yourself, so it stays private and simple.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Savings challenges already have huge search and social demand: people print paper trackers and post their progress. StackUp turns that into an app with streaks and milestones, has real subscription potential, and is the pilot app for the growth engine because its content (saving tips, challenges, progress) is endless and evergreen.

## Who it's for

Young adults and anyone saving for a holiday, emergency fund, house deposit or a specific purchase.

**The problem:** Saving feels abstract and slow. Paper trackers get lost and there's no sense of momentum.

## Version one

- Prebuilt challenges: 52-week, £5-a-day, save £500/£1,000/£5,000, £1,000 in 90 days, 100 envelopes, no-spend weekends
- Log a deposit in one tap; the progress bar and stack fill up
- Weekly streak with earned freezes, plus milestones (first £100, halfway, done)
- Custom challenge (target and deadline) with a suggested weekly amount
- Shareable milestone and completion cards

**Example:** A user starts '£1,000 in 90 days', logs £20 here and £15 there, keeps their weekly streak alive with one freeze, and shares a card when they pass £500.

## Later

## How it makes money

| Model | Detail |
|---|---|
| Free | One active challenge, with ads |
| Premium | £2.99/month, £14.99/year or £6.99 lifetime: unlimited goals, custom challenges, themes |
| Ads | Banner only, never on the deposit screen |

## Why people come back

Built on **The Progression Loop**, the portfolio's shared retention framework
(streaks with forgiveness, visible progress, milestones, recaps, no guilt-trips).

| Stage | In StackUp |
|---|---|
| Cue | Payday, plus a weekly 'stack day' the user picks |
| Tiny win | Log a deposit in one tap |
| Progress | Progress bar, weekly streak, milestones |
| Share | Milestone and completion cards |
| Next step | On completion, immediately offer the next challenge (bigger, or seasonal) |
| Cadence | Weekly + payday |

## Watch-outs

Users leave after finishing a challenge unless the next one is offered straight away. It's a tracker, not financial advice, and the wording must stay that way.
