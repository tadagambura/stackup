# StackUp

**Savings challenges you actually finish**

> #1 of 33 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Daily or weekly, per challenge · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium–high · Money & Work
> **Build first · Growth & Maintenance Engine pilot**

## What it is

Pick a savings challenge (52-week, £5-a-day, £1,000 in 90 days, 100 envelopes, no-spend weekends), log what you put away, and watch the stack grow. No bank connection: you log deposits yourself, so it stays private and simple.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Savings challenges already have huge search and social demand: people print paper trackers and post their progress. StackUp turns that into an app with streaks and milestones, has real subscription potential, and is the pilot app for the Growth & Maintenance Engine because its content (saving tips, challenges, progress) is endless and evergreen.

## Who it's for

Young adults and anyone saving for a holiday, emergency fund, house deposit or a specific purchase.

**The problem:** Saving feels abstract and slow. Paper trackers get lost and there's no sense of momentum.

## Version one

- Prebuilt challenges: 52-week, £5-a-day, save £500/£1,000/£5,000, £1,000 in 90 days, 100 envelopes, no-spend weekends
- Log a deposit in one tap; the progress bar and stack fill up
- Weekly streak with earned freezes, plus milestones (first £100, halfway, done)
- Custom challenge (target and deadline) with a suggested weekly amount
- Shareable milestone and completion cards

**Example:** A user starts '£1,000 in 90 days', logs £20 here and £15 there, keeps their weekly streak alive with one freeze, and shares a card when they pass £500.

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Deposit: a coin drops onto the stack with a clink and the stickman fist-pumps
- Every £100: the stack wobbles, topples into a confetti pile, and the stickman dives in like a ball pit
- Streak freeze used: the stickman wraps the stack in a scarf and hands it a hot chocolate
- Challenge complete: the stack grows into a skyscraper and a tiny stickman climbs it waving a £ flag
- Opening the app on payday: the piggy bank moonwalks across the screen

**Line we don't cross:** No drama about withdrawing or missing a deposit.

## AI

**None.** Not needed. Plain code does everything this app does.

## Watch-outs

Users leave after finishing a challenge unless the next one is offered straight away. It's a tracker, not financial advice, and the wording must stay that way.
