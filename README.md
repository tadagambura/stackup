# StackUp

**Know what's safe to spend, then stack the rest**

> #1 of 17 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Daily · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium–high · Money & Work
> **Build first · Growth & Maintenance Engine pilot**

## What it is

Two questions, one money app. First: what can I safely spend before payday? Set your pay schedule and bills, and StackUp shows safe-to-spend for today, this week and until payday, with an 'can I afford £X?' check and every subscription and renewal in one place. Second: how do I actually save? Pick a challenge (52-week, £5-a-day, 100 envelopes, £1,000 in 90 days, no-spend weekends) and stack money in one tap; anything left before payday can be stacked too. Every day there's also a one-minute money lesson or quiz with XP and levels. No bank linking: your numbers stay on your phone.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Safe-to-spend and savings challenges serve the same person at the same moment, so they work better as one app: 'you've got £14 left before payday, stack £5 of it?' turns leftovers into savings. Standalone challenge trackers are mostly free, which makes them hard to charge for, while Emma charges £4.99 to £14.99 a month for safe-to-spend, bills and subscriptions with a bank link. StackUp offers the useful parts without linking a bank, for much less. Savings challenges are huge on TikTok (cash stuffing has 3B+ views) yet the challenge apps themselves are tiny: an opening.

## Who it's for

UK young adults and anyone living payday to payday who wants to stop running out of money and finally save something, without handing an app their bank login.

**The problem:** Money runs out before payday, subscriptions sneak up, and saving feels impossible or boring.

## Version one

- Pay schedule (weekly, fortnightly, four-weekly, monthly or irregular), bills and safe-to-spend for today, this week and until payday
- 'Can I afford £X?' check and payday countdown
- Subscriptions and renewals: monthly total, trial-ending and renewal reminders
- Savings challenges: 52-week, £5-a-day, 100 envelopes, £1, 000 in 90 days, no-spend weekends; one-tap deposits; stack your leftover before payday
- One 60-second money lesson or quiz a day with XP, levels and a forgiving streak; every lesson shows its source and when it was checked

**Example:** A user starts '£1,000 in 90 days', does the morning lesson on emergency funds (+10 XP), logs £20, keeps a 40-day streak with one freeze, and shares a card when they pass £500.

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Deposit: coins clink onto the stack
- Safe-to-spend hits £0: he empties his pockets, a fly buzzes out, he scratches his head
- Daily lesson: right answer = backflip
- Payday: confetti and a happy dance
- Challenge complete: the flag goes on top of the stack

**Line we don't cross:** Never shame spending; money warnings stay calm and kind.

## AI

**None for facts.** Lessons are written and checked by people from trusted sources (GOV.UK, MoneyHelper, HMRC, FCA). AI may help draft, but never publishes a fact unreviewed.

## Watch-outs

Lessons must stay general education, never personal financial advice (FCA financial promotions): sources shown and reviewed. Safe-to-spend is only as good as what people enter, so the app says so plainly. Bigger scope than a simple tracker: ship challenges and lessons first, safe-to-spend in the next update.
