# StackUp

**Save money and learn money, one minute a day**

> #1 of 16 · Wave 1: Daily keepers · Used: Daily · Very easy · Revenue potential: Medium–high · Money & Work
> **Build first · Growth & Maintenance Engine pilot**

## What it is

Pick a savings challenge (52-week, £5-a-day, £1,000 in 90 days, 100 envelopes, no-spend weekends), log what you put away and watch the stack grow. Every day there's also a one-minute money lesson or quiz (budgeting, saving, credit, pensions, tax, scams) with XP and levels, inspired by the ideas in Morgan Housel's The Psychology of Money and checked against trusted UK sources. Learn something, then do something: each lesson ends with an action in your challenge. No bank connection; you log deposits yourself.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Saving and understanding money are the same habit. Savings challenges already have huge search and social demand, and a daily lesson gives a reason to open the app every day even when there's nothing to deposit, which is the strongest retention driver in the portfolio. One app instead of two means one brand to grow, one store listing and twice the reasons to come back. Still the pilot app for the Growth & Maintenance Engine: its content (saving tips, challenges, money lessons) is endless and evergreen.

## Who it's for

Young adults who are saving for something and feel behind on money basics.

**The problem:** Saving feels abstract and slow, and money advice is long and overwhelming. Paper trackers get lost and nobody explains the 'why'.

## Version one

- Prebuilt challenges: 52-week, £5-a-day, save £500/£1,000/£5,000, £1,000 in 90 days, 100 envelopes, no-spend weekends
- Log a deposit in one tap; the progress bar and stack fill up
- One 60-second lesson or quiz a day, with XP, levels and a streak (earned freezes)
- Units: money mindset (inspired by The Psychology of Money), budgeting, saving, credit, pensions, tax, scams; every lesson shows its source and when it was checked
- Milestone and completion cards to share

**Example:** A user starts '£1,000 in 90 days', does the morning lesson on emergency funds (+10 XP), logs £20, keeps a 40-day streak with one freeze, and shares a card when they pass £500.

## Animation and tone: five signature animations

**Playful.** Celebrations, confetti, cute characters, satisfying sounds and mild comic reactions. Nothing tragic.

- Deposit: a coin drops onto the stack with a clink and the stickman fist-pumps
- Lesson right: the coin mascot does a backflip and '+10 XP' floats up (wrong: a kind 'ooh, close!')
- Every £100: the stack wobbles, topples into a confetti pile, and the stickman dives in
- Streak freeze used: the stickman wraps the stack in a scarf and hands it a hot chocolate
- Challenge complete: the tower grows and the stickman throws a flag onto the top

**Line we don't cross:** No drama about withdrawing or missing a deposit.

## AI

**None for facts.** Lessons are written and checked by people from trusted sources (GOV.UK, MoneyHelper, HMRC, FCA). AI may help draft, but never publishes a fact unreviewed.

## Watch-outs

Lessons must be accurate, sourced, reviewed by a person and re-checked every April; AI can help draft but never publishes unreviewed. The Psychology of Money is used for its ideas, credited, never copied. Education and tracking, not personal advice: FCA financial-promotion rules mean no product recommendations. Users can leave after finishing a challenge, so the next challenge and the daily lesson must always be ready.
